## 🛡️ AdBloat Remover (AdShield & Security)

A modern Android debloater, adware scanner, and display fixer built with **Jetpack Compose** and **Material 3**.

### Download Latest Version

- **Latest Version**: `v1.0.0`
- **Direct Download Link**: [Download adbloat-remover.apk](https://github.com/jerrykgm/apps/raw/main/adbloat-remover.apk)
- **Versioned Link**: [Download adbloat-remover-v1.0.0.apk](https://github.com/jerrykgm/apps/raw/main/adbloat-remover-v1.0.0.apk)

### Key Features
- **System Bloatware & Telemetry Manager**: Identifies preloaded system bloatware across Samsung (*Bixby, AR Zone, DiagMon, Kids*), Xiaomi (*MSA ads, Analytics, Daemon, Joyose, Mi Pay*), and the **Facebook Suite** (*katana, system, appmanager, services*).
- **Active vs. Disabled Detection**: Scans and categorizes apps that are currently active vs. already disabled on your phone so you can review or safely re-enable them.
- **Safety Ratings**: Clear **"Safe to Disable"** and **"Caution"** badges ensure you never break critical phone features.
- **Rogue Launcher & Adware Detector**: Scans your default launcher to stop fake launchers from popping up ads when opening YouTube or other apps. Detects apps with overlay permissions (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`).
- **1-Tap Private DNS AdBlocker**: Copies `dns.adguard-dns.com` and directly launches your phone's network settings for system-wide ad protection.
- **Amazon & Dark Mode Display Fixer**: Resolves the infamous Amazon Shopping black card and unreadable black text glitch caused by forced dark mode.
- **OEM Helpers**: Restores hidden Samsung Galaxy Themes and switches to Apple-style swipe gesture navigation.
- **1-Tap ADB Debloat Script Export**: Generates and copies ready-to-paste batch commands (`adb shell pm disable-user --user 0 <pkg>`) for quick USB debloating.
